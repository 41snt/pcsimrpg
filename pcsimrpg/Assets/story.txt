Chapter 1: HDD Region

The player is part of a powerful group called the Monarch Components. He is the leader of this group and serves as the Motherboard Monarch. The group consists of the Processor Monarch, GPU Monarch, RAM Monarch, PSU Monarch, and Hard Drive Monarch.

For many years, the Monarchs ruled over their regions with absolute power.

One day, the Motherboard Monarch meets someone who changes everything. A woman who sees him not as a ruler or a weapon, but as a person. They spend time together, and slowly the darkness inside him fades. Through her, he begins to understand something he never had before.

Peace.

Because of this, he makes a decision that shocks the Monarchs.

He steps down from his position as leader and passes the title to the second strongest member — the Processor Monarch.

The other Monarchs are furious. They cannot believe their leader would abandon everything for a human woman. They threaten to kill her if he walks away.

But the Motherboard Monarch stands firm.

“If anything happens to her,” he tells them, “I will wipe every one of you out.”

Seeing the resolve in his eyes, the Monarchs reluctantly allow him to leave.

Years pass.

The former Motherboard Monarch now lives quietly as a human. He owns and operates a PC repair shop, spending his days building computers, troubleshooting systems, and helping customers.

For the first time in his existence, life is simple.

Until one evening.

His wife disappears.

A day passes with no trace of her. Panic begins to take hold. He checks security cameras, searches the city, and asks everyone he knows.

Nothing.

Until he reviews the CCTV footage again.

There it is.

A familiar light.

A signal he recognizes instantly.

The HDD Monarch.

Fury ignites within him. Without hesitation, he rushes toward the HDD Region and demands answers.

The HDD Monarch says nothing.

Enraged, the player attacks.

But he is still human.

The weakest Monarch easily defeats him. He is beaten and thrown out of the region like trash.

Broken and humiliated, he returns home.

That night, he makes a painful choice.

Beneath his PC shop lies a hidden chamber containing the sealed power of the Motherboard Monarch.

He promised his wife he would never use that power again.

But now he has no choice.

He breaks the seal.

And the hunt begins.

However, reclaiming his power comes with severe consequences. His human body cannot handle the full strength of a Monarch.

If he uses his power during the day, his body will collapse.

Fully restoring his Monarch form would take nearly ten years.

And if the community discovers that he is a Monarch, his family would be banished from the region.

So he continues running his PC shop during the day.

At night, he becomes something else.

A hunter.

That night he returns to the HDD Region.

This time he slaughters every guard who stands in his way.

When he reaches the Hard Drive Monarch, the battle is short.

The player overwhelms him and absorbs the HDD component, gaining access to its vast data storage and analytical abilities.

The player demands to know where his wife is.

Instead of answering, the HDD Monarch activates his self-destruct.

The explosion engulfs the chamber.

But it fails to even scratch the player.

Then he realizes something terrifying.

The sun will rise in less than thirty seconds.

He shuts down his power and escapes the region just before dawn.

The next morning the shop is overflowing with customers.

While repairing computers and assembling systems, one customer walks toward him.

It is the Processor Monarch.

As he looks around, he realizes something horrifying.

Every single customer in the shop is being controlled by her.

Using their bodies like puppets.

She calmly delivers an ultimatum.

If he does not return to the Monarchs within one month, his wife will be merged into a component that will replace him as the Motherboard.

Then she disappears into the crowd.

The customers suddenly return to normal.

The shop falls silent.

The player stands behind the counter, hands trembling.

One month.

Thirty nights.

Chapter 2: GPU Region

That night, strange disturbances begin appearing across the city. Screens flicker. Security cameras glitch. Digital billboards distort violently.

Following the signal interference leads him to the GPU Region.

The moment he enters, reality begins to shift.

Buildings warp. Streets bend. The sky flickers like a corrupted display.

The GPU Monarch is manipulating visual reality itself.

Suddenly hundreds of broken GPU parts surge toward him like a metallic storm.

He fights for hours.

Eventually the GPU Monarch appears, clapping slowly.

The Motherboard Monarch asks him,

“Is this what you do to your people?”

The GPU Monarch shakes his head.

“No. Those creatures… are not my doing.”

Then he points.

The player turns.

His wife is standing there.

Alive.

He sprints toward her, heart pounding.

But just before reaching her, the GPU Monarch kicks him back and locks him in a crushing hold.

The player struggles.

Nothing works.

Then he remembers the HDD component inside him.

He activates it.

The drive spins violently as thousands of combat possibilities run through his mind like simulations.

He analyzes the lock.

Finds the weakness.

Breaks free.

But the GPU Monarch knocks him unconscious.

When he wakes, the fight resumes.

His energy is nearly gone.

The GPU Monarch prepares the final strike.

But the HDD component activates again, replaying every second of the battle inside his mind.

Suddenly the player moves.

Perfectly.

Every attack is predicted.

Every movement countered.

The GPU Monarch realizes too late.

The player destroys his core.

But the Monarch rises again.

His body rotting and twisting.

The same infection the player saw earlier spreads through him.

Before the corrupted Monarch can attack, the virus destabilizes his body.

He collapses and disintegrates.

The player now knows something far worse exists beyond the Monarchs.

Chapter 3: PSU Region

The next night he enters the PSU Region, a massive industrial wasteland of generators and power grids.

Inside, his energy begins draining immediately.

This place feeds on power.

He fights through waves of enemies until reaching the Power Grid Core.

There the PSU Monarch waits.

The player demands information about his wife.

The PSU Monarch offers a deal.

Return to the Monarchs.

The player refuses.

A massive high-voltage battle begins.

Electricity erupts across the battlefield as the grid overloads.

After a brutal fight, the player defeats the PSU Monarch and absorbs the PSU component, granting him limitless energy output.

But while leaving the region, he sees another rotting creature wandering through the city.

The infection is spreading.

Chapter 4: Underground Pit → RAM Region

Back at the shop, the player works nonstop repairing PCs in a speed-based mini game.

Suddenly he is short-circuited and dragged into an underground PC Overclocking Arena.

There he meets Max, another PC shop owner whose wife was captured by the arena boss.

The player wins the competition by using dry ice instead of liquid nitrogen, outsmarting his opponent.

During the fight he realizes something.

Only a Monarch could disable his abilities.

The RAM Monarch.

After escaping the arena, he discovers it is secretly located inside the RAM Region.

The RAM Monarch attacks using memory blades and endless clones.

Using GPU perception and PSU power, the player detects the real one and destroys her.

He claims the RAM component.

Now he is ready.

Only one Monarch remains.

Chapter 5: CPU Region (Final)

The CPU Region is eerily peaceful.

Thousands of citizens move in perfect synchronization.

The Processor Monarch controls them all.

She reveals the truth.

Two months ago, a virus from another planet crashed between the GPU and PSU regions.

It corrupts machines and living beings alike.

The monsters he has seen were infected victims.

Entire regions are already lost.

Her plan is total system integration.

Merge all Monarch components under her control to destroy the virus.

But that would sacrifice humanity.

His wife appears on a screen.

She begs him not to let the Processor control the merge.

The merge itself isn’t the problem.

Control is.

Realizing this, the player attacks.

The final battle begins.

After a devastating fight, the Processor Monarch becomes infected by the virus.

His wife escapes and helps him fight.

Together they defeat the corrupted Processor.

But the merge chamber is collapsing.

Knowing the virus will destroy everything, his wife sacrifices herself and inserts the CPU component into him.

Light fills the chamber.

The merge begins.

And the world changes forever.
